Programme overview

You practise before you treat.

Dentistry at AKUHS is a five-year degree that prepares you to practise as a general dental practitioner. The first two years build the biomedical and dental sciences — anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, dental anatomy, dental materials and oral histopathology — with simulated dentistry starting in Year 2. Year 3 brings the move from the simulation bench to treating your first patients under supervision, and the final two years are given to comprehensive patient care across restorative dentistry, prosthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, oral surgery, orthodontics, and paediatric and geriatric dentistry.

The Simulation Centre gives every student their own phantom-head unit paired with a dental operating microscope, so magnification-assisted dentistry becomes second nature from Year 2, and a dedicated Digital Dentistry Laboratory carries you through intra-oral scanning, CAD/CAM design, 3D printing and computer-guided implant planning. Across Years 3 to 5 you complete around 1,920 hours of supervised clinical training in the Dental Teaching Clinic and at Ain Al Khaleej Hospital — well beyond the national minimum.

Goals and objectives

Programme goals

  • Goal 1

    Strengthen dental education and service capacity in Al Ain and support greater regional healthcare self-sufficiency.

  • Goal 2

    Increase Emirati participation in the oral healthcare workforce, where nationals currently represent a relatively small proportion of licensed dentists.

  • Goal 3

    Equip dental graduates with future skills by integrating digital technologies and artificial intelligence into dental education and clinical practice.

Programme objectives

  1. Develop competent general dental practitioners capable of improving oral health outcomes in Al Ain and across the Emirate of Abu Dhabi.
  2. Support the recruitment, development, retention and successful integration of UAE nationals into the dental profession.
  3. Prepare graduates to pursue research, postgraduate education, specialty training and clinical-academic careers.
  4. Enable graduates to critically evaluate, effectively apply and ethically govern digital technologies and artificial intelligence in dental practice.
  5. Equip graduates with future-ready, transferable and innovative professional skills, together with a commitment to lifelong learning, enabling them to adapt to and lead changes in dentistry, technology and healthcare systems.
  6. Strengthen collaboration with industry, healthcare partners and local authorities to support workforce development, dental education, innovation and research.

Transfer applicants and recognition of prior learning

AKUHS may consider applications from students currently enrolled at, or who have completed coursework at, another recognised higher education institution and who wish to join an AKUHS programme with advanced standing.

  • Transfer applicants must satisfy all university-wide and programme-specific admission requirements.
  • The prior institution must be recognised by the UAE Ministry of Education and the Commission for Academic Accreditation, or by an equivalent foreign accreditation authority recognised by the UAE Ministry of Education.
  • A minimum CGPA of 2.0 on a 4.0 scale (or established equivalent) is required at the prior institution.
  • Applicants must be in good academic and disciplinary standing, evidenced by an official Letter of Good Standing, and must submit official transcripts of all prior higher education coursework.
  • No more than 50% of the programme credits may be transferred.
  • Credit is transferred only for courses passed at grade C or above (course GPA 2.0 on a 4.0 scale) and only where the receiving College determines that content, learning outcomes and credit weighting are substantially equivalent.
  • Credit is not transferred for clinical courses except with the express approval of the College Dean and on documented demonstration of competence.

Recognition of prior learning is governed by the AKUHS Recognition of Prior Learning Policy, which sets out the assessment process, evidence requirements and appeal rights.

Career paths

The Bachelor of Dental Surgery is designed to satisfy the regulatory eligibility requirements for licensure as a general dental practitioner in the United Arab Emirates, while building the academic, research, leadership and digital foundations required for subsequent specialty training, academic careers, research, public oral health and innovation roles.

The route to UAE licensure

Graduating from the programme does not by itself confer a licence to practise. After graduation, you must complete a structured one-year supervised dental internship, which is a precondition for general dental practitioner licensure in the UAE. The internship is regulated by the National Institute of Health Specialities (NIHS). It is undertaken through the AKUHS Internship Office, sits outside the five-year programme, and does not carry credit within the 206 credit hours. Following completion of the internship and satisfaction of the relevant licensing examination requirements, graduates are eligible to apply for licensure as general dental practitioners with one of the UAE regulatory authorities and to register for independent practice within the scope defined by UAE law and regulation.

Where graduates work

Roles for which the degree, followed by internship and licensure, prepares you directly

  • General dental practice — the primary destination for most graduates, spanning government-sector clinics, private dental practices, multispecialty group practices and corporate dental networks.
  • Government and primary-care oral health services, community and school oral-health services.

Roles that require further training, postgraduate study or additional registration

  • Specialist practice — accessible through postgraduate specialty residencies or master’s-level programmes in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopaedics, endodontics, periodontology, prosthodontics, oral and maxillofacial surgery, paediatric dentistry, oral medicine and oral pathology, oral and maxillofacial radiology, dental public health, restorative and aesthetic dentistry, and digital dentistry and dental informatics.
  • Academic and educational careers, typically following postgraduate specialty training combined with a master’s or doctoral research degree.
  • Research and innovation roles, including research fellowships, hospital-based clinical research and postgraduate research degrees (MSc, MPhil, PhD).
  • Public and community oral health leadership roles with the Ministry of Health and Prevention, emirate-level health authorities, public health units and national oral-health programmes.
  • Practice ownership, dental entrepreneurship and healthcare management, supported within the curriculum by Health Information Systems, Dental Practice Management and Entrepreneurship, Innovation & Sustainability.
  • Careers at the intersection of dentistry and technology, including digital dentistry consultancy, dental software development, AI-enabled diagnostic ventures and CAD/CAM dental laboratories.

Practising outside the UAE

The programme is designed to align with international competency frameworks, which supports the international portability of the qualification. Graduates wishing to practise abroad will require examinations and registration in the destination jurisdiction, and eligibility is determined by the authorities of that jurisdiction.

Support for your career

The Employability and Alumni Relations Office, which incorporates the Internship Office, supports students and graduates with guidance on regulatory pathways, documentation and licensure examination preparation, employer liaison including an annual dental careers fair, and liaison with Nafis and the Emirati Talent Competitiveness Council for Emirati graduates. AKUHS also offers alumni continuing professional development and continuing access to selected institutional learning resources.

Financial aid

AKUHS operates a structured framework of tuition discounts, set out in the Tuition and Fee Structure Policy, which rewards academic merit, supports family enrolment and encourages prompt full payment. These are reductions in tuition; they are distinct from the instalment plan described under Tuition Fees, which spreads payment but does not reduce the amount owed.

12%of annual tuition

Chairman Discount

Eligibility. Newly admitted students only, at the point of fresh entry into an AKUHS programme. Students who have previously been enrolled at AKUHS, who have transferred internally, or who are returning after withdrawal are not eligible. Applies across all programmes.

Continuation and renewal. Renewable in subsequent years only if the student achieves and maintains a CGPA of 3.8 or above at the end of the preceding academic year. A student whose CGPA falls below 3.8 forfeits the discount for the following year and is liable for full annual tuition. Reinstatement is not retroactive but may be reconsidered at a later annual review if the threshold is met again.

5%of annual tuition

Sibling Discount

Eligibility. The second sibling concurrently enrolled at AKUHS. It does not extend to the first enrolled sibling. Both siblings must be enrolled concurrently in the academic year claimed, and documentary evidence of the relationship must be submitted to the Admission and Registrar’s Office when the discount is applied for.

Continuation and renewal. Applies for each academic year in which both siblings are concurrently enrolled.

3%of annual tuition

Full-Payment Discount

Eligibility. Students or sponsors who pay the full annual tuition in a single payment. Payment must be received and cleared no later than the day before the official start of the Fall semester for the relevant academic year.

Continuation and renewal. Applies per academic year and must be re-elected each year. Where a student pays in full and subsequently withdraws, the refund provisions of the Tuition and Fee Structure Policy apply.

Combining discounts

Where more than one discount applies, the discounts are applied to annual tuition in this order: first the Chairman Discount, then the Sibling Discount, then the Full-Payment Discount. Each successive discount is calculated on the balance remaining after the preceding one. Discounts under this Policy cannot be combined with externally funded scholarships or third-party sponsor agreements that already provide a tuition reduction. All discounts are conditional on continuing verification of eligibility by the Registrar’s Office and the Finance Office.

The combined effect of all institutional discounts is capped at 20% of annual tuition. Where the sequential calculation above would produce a larger reduction, the reduction is limited to that cap.

Sponsorship

Students funded by a government body, employer, foundation or other institutional sponsor may have AKUHS invoice the sponsor directly, on receipt of an official sponsorship letter meeting the requirements of the Tuition and Fee Structure Policy. Ultimate financial liability for all fees remains with the student. Where sponsorship already provides a tuition reduction, the non-combination rule above applies.
